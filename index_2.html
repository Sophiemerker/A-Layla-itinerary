Trip logic

A capital, the mountains, then the sea.

Every good trip has a shape. This one opens in a capital city thick with temples and street food, climbs into cool mountain air for elephants and markets, then eases down onto the coast for real rest. Three stops, three chapters, three hotel changes in total. Along the way: one ethical elephant encounter, one private boat day through limestone islands, and one cooking class with a market walk built in. Everything else stays open. No day is filled from morning to evening.

City & culture

Temples, a floating market, and a modern art stop in Bangkok.

02

Food

Michelin listed street stalls, Chinatown seafood, and the definitive khao soi. The Michelin Guide has quietly become one of the most reliable ways to eat well in Thailand, and we leaned on it throughout.

03

Nature & a boat

An ethical elephant sanctuary and a private longtail charter through Krabi's islands.

04

Rest

Five nights in Ao Nang, built around one private boat day. For a second dose of movement, Tiger Cave Temple's summit climb is twenty minutes away. Otherwise, the rest stays open on purpose.

Positioned by real latitude and longitude, not a generic map. Chiang Mai sits north, Krabi sits south on the Andaman side, Bangkok holds the middle and does double duty as the connection point on both ends.

Before you go

General notes

Weather

Just past the rainy season, both north and south. Warm and humid, 28 to 33°C, with a chance of short afternoon showers, more likely in Krabi than Chiang Mai. A light rain jacket beats an umbrella only plan.

Currency

Thai Baht. Cards are widely accepted in hotels and restaurants, carry cash for markets, street food, and temple entry fees.

Visa

German passport holders currently get visa exempt entry for tourism, up to 60 days. Worth a quick check closer to departure in case rules shift.

Tipping

Not obligatory. Five to ten percent at sit down restaurants and rounding up for massages and drivers is appreciated and increasingly normal.

Temple dress code

Shoulders and knees covered at Wat Pho, Wat Arun, Doi Suthep, and the rest. A light scarf or sarong each is enough.

eSIM

Airalo, roughly €14 for 10GB. Install before landing so you are connected the moment you touch down.

Health

No mandatory vaccinations for Thailand from Germany. Standard travel insurance and a basic first aid kit are sensible. Tap water is not drinkable, bottled or filtered throughout.
